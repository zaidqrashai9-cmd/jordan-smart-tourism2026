# Petra name and role references — Windows 1.6

Translation now performs a bounded English Petra lookup before the first translation of the edited source. Enter your Gemini key as before. Click فحص الأسماء من بترا (Translate also starts this check). Review the evidence window, then click Translate and confirm identity/title review.

Gemini proposes search transliterations for up to 8 names explicitly present in Arabic. Those proposals are NOT verified. Google News RSS discovers up to 5 recent results per person. Only allowlisted Petra English hosts/pages are fetched. The model extracts an exact spelling, office and verbatim quote. Code requires the name and office to occur inside that quote and the quote to occur in the fetched article, with a readable publication date. It chooses the newest dated match found, flags same-date office conflicts, and shows unverified names separately.

This is NOT exhaustive Petra search or proof of a person's current office. Human identity review is required, especially similar names, transliteration variation and old role descriptions. The source article's office must not be silently replaced; the model is instructed to flag conflicts. References override generic spelling guidance, subject to human review. Date/role evidence is shown separately from the English draft. Changed source text forces a fresh lookup. References are session-only; no persistent verified glossary is claimed.

Petra pages, Google RSS or article decoding can be unavailable. In that case no name is marked verified; user may choose to translate after reviewing the unverified warning. Multiple Gemini requests consume the account's free quota and can be slow. No search-grounding tool or paid fallback is enabled. Key remains process memory only. No live authenticated end-to-end test was possible without the user's private key. Tests cover allowed source URLs, exact evidence, newest-date sorting, same-date conflicts, and existing Gemini behaviors.
